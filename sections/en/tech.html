A family's voice deserves more care

We use only what we need,
and we do not keep it long

A voice identifies a person. So we decided up front what we keep and what we delete.

01 Voice data

Original recordings are deleted

What remains is match data and nothing else. That data cannot be turned back into a voice.

02 Family data

It stays inside the family

Who enrolled and who is connected is visible to your connected family only. No other family and no outsider can look it up.

03 Scope of analysis

Used only to confirm family

We look at two things: whether the voice matches an enrolled family member, and whether it was made by AI. We do not look at what was said.

Privacy is not an option buried behind a setting. It is how the service is built.
