No technology stops every voice-phishing call. IsFam does one thing: it confirms a family member's voice. It is there to help you decide, and it does not replace an official finding by the police or your bank.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which calls do you analyse?

Calls from an enrolled family member's number, and calls where an unknown number claims to be a family member.

If the verdict is Safe, can I trust it completely?

No. If money or personal details are involved, call your family member back on the number you have saved.

Is the original audio kept?

No. Once the analysis is done the original is deleted, and only a form that cannot be reversed remains.

Can an older parent use it on their own?

Yes. Enrolment and connecting the family go much more smoothly with help, though.

Can it detect an AI voice?

Yes. Alongside matching against the enrolled family member, it also checks whether the voice was synthesised by AI.
