How it works

Enrol once,
checked on every call

01
Setup

Record a voice,
then connect your family

01Record

Three lines, in your normal voice

Read the three sentences on screen. It takes about 16 seconds, and it becomes your family's own voiceprint.

02Enrol

The app handles the rest

What you read is turned into match data. There is nothing for you to do. It takes a few seconds.

03Invite

One link and you are connected

Send an invitation by message or QR code. Whoever receives it only has to tap the link once. That is the whole setup — from here on the checking happens on its own.

02
Call analysis

No confusing scores —
a result you can act on

We do not show you a similarity score to interpret. Every call ends in one of three verdicts, and each one tells you what to do right now.

Safe

This is your
enrolled family member

It matches the enrolled voice, with no sign of AI synthesis.

Carry on with the call
Verify

We cannot
be sure yet

Call quality was poor, or the signals disagree.

Call back on the saved number
Danger

This is not your
enrolled family member

It does not match, or it carries clear marks of AI synthesis.

Hang up and stop any transfer
03
Family alert

One person notices,
the whole family is safer

The moment something looks wrong, one tap tells the rest of the family and stops the scam spreading to the next person.

One-tap alert

No forms, no steps. The person on the call taps Alert and every connected family member's phone goes off at once.

Stops the second call

Family members who hear about it in real time are ready when the scammer moves on to the next person.

Nothing sensitive leaves

The alert carries no call content and no phone numbers. None of it is stored on a server.
